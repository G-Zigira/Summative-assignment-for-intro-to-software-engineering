# Trails (v0.4.0)
Places (every scene of the two story trails has its own picture): Lion's Path = Kangaba hall, the forge, the exile plains, Kirina. Web of Wisdom = the forest, the farm village, the big tree, the village square. The tutorial keeps the courtyard, wall garden and road.

1. **Tutorial: Trail 1** teaches walking, talking to the Elder, choices and story pieces.
2. **The Lion's Path** (Sundiata): Kangaba courtyard -> wall garden -> road -> Kirina. Elder: Jeli Balla (griot).
3. **The Web of Wisdom** (Anansi): forest -> wall garden -> big tree -> courtyard. Elders: Nana Yaa, Anansi, Ntikuma.
Finales: The Lion's Path ends with the **Horse Charge** battle; The Web of Wisdom ends with **Wisdom Catch**. All three minigames are also in the title screen's Mini Games menu.
Convention: an Elder walks the player through each scene; touchable objects (star marker) each add a short story piece; moving between areas plays the runner.
**Status: trails 2 and 3 are drafts written from the story outline and need fact-checking by a historian/elder (see docs/CONTENT-GUIDELINES.md) before release.** Edit text in `src/data/trails/*.json` and `src/data/codex.json`.
